Something cool ✨

You found it! Return of the Imposter is a game my friends and I built in C on a custom physics engine, compiled to WebAssembly so it runs right here in your browser. You're an imposter on a spaceship: platform through the levels, dodge (or eliminate) crewmates, and reach the reactor.

Return of the Imposter

A fun blend of Subway Surfers set in the Among Us universe. Best played on a computer with a keyboard.

controls · WASD to move · click anywhere to aim the tongue · P to pause · R to restart level

Agent Academy 🐣

Hatch and train your own tiny agent! It learns by reinforcement learning, live in your browser. Place walls and treats to shape its world, watch it explore, and peek inside its brain to see what it has learned. Graduate it through all three levels.
