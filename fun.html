Return of the Imposter

A fun blend of Subway Surfers set in the Among Us universe. Best played on a computer with a keyboard.

The goal

You're an imposter on a spaceship. Platform through each level and reach the vent at the end to advance; your ultimate mission is to reach the reactor and destroy the ship.

Crewmates attack when you're in their line of sight, and hitting them or obstacles costs hearts (see your health bar, top left). Lose them all and the level restarts.

Your secret weapon is your tongue. Click anywhere on the screen and your tongue shoots straight to that spot. It acts like a spring: it latches onto whatever you clicked (a wall, a platform, a crewmate) and pulls you toward it, launching you across gaps and up ledges. Tongue a crewmate to eliminate them. And the black holes? Actually trampolines that fling you forward with momentum.

Controls

  • Mouse to navigate the main menu and pick a level
  • W A S D to move and jump
  • Click anywhere to aim and shoot your tongue
  • P to pause and return to the menu
  • R to restart the level

Best played on a computer with a keyboard.

Agent Academy

Train your own tiny AI! The little blue creature below learns by reinforcement learning: it tries things, earns rewards when something works, and slowly figures out the best strategy through trial and error.

press train to start your agent's education

The goal

Your agent (the glowing blue creature) needs to learn to reach the yellow star. It starts out knowing nothing, so it wanders randomly. Every attempt is one episode: reaching the star earns it a big reward, and over many episodes it figures out the best route on its own. That's reinforcement learning!

Each time it finds the star quickly, its streak grows. A streak of 6 means it has mastered the level and moves up a grade. Help it master all 3 levels and it graduates.

Watch out: the red discs are hazards that end the episode on contact. Your agent will learn to avoid them.

How to play

  • Press ▶ train and watch it stumble, then improve
  • Click the grid to shape its world: add walls to make the maze harder, drop treats (bonus snacks worth +5) to lure it along a path, or erase to clear a square
  • Drag the speed slider to slow training down and watch each step, or speed it up
  • Press peek inside its brain to see what it has learned: squares glow green where it expects good things and red where it expects trouble
