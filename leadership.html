Camp Venom: Free Python Coding Camp

Founder & Director · 2021 to present

I founded Camp Venom, a youth-led program that teaches kids in Minnesota how to code for free, through interactive projects and games. The camp has taught 100+ students with 50+ volunteers, and helped boost CS class sizes at Minnesota schools by 10%. Campers went on to pursue CS in college as first-generation students. Every summer I mentor a new group of student directors so the camp keeps running, year after year.

Dine & Dialogue · Prompted by Us: AI & Creativity

In collaboration with the Caltech Center for Inclusion & Diversity · 45+ attendees

Exploring how Gemini (generative AI) can support creativity (and learning) without replacing the human voice behind it. Together, we discussed how AI is showing up in our classes, creative projects, and communities; where it belongs in the creative process; and how bias shapes what AI produces. The goal: to think about AI not as a replacement for creativity, but as a tool for expanding and expressing ideas that begin with us.
